About Professor Islam

Professor Dr. Md. Nurul Islam is a distinguished geographer and academic leader with over two decades of teaching, research, and administrative experience. His expertise spans climate change, floodplain management, GIS, remote sensing, and sustainable development.

As Vice Chancellor of Dhaka Central University, he is guiding a major academic transformation that blends seven established colleges into a modern, research-driven university focused on national progress.

  • Education: PhD Geography, University of Nottingham, UK
  • Research Focus: Climate change, environmental systems, agricultural resilience
  • Leadership: Vice Chancellor, Dhaka Central University
  • Academic Roles: Professor, Department of Geography & Environment, Jahangirnagar University
